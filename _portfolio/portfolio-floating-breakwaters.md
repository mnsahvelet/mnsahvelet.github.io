**Wave Transmission by Floating Breakwaters using SPH-based Numerical Analysis**

Muhammed N. Sahvelet, Cary D. Troy, Yue (Olivia) Meng — *Purdue University, Lyles School of
Civil and Construction Engineering.*

Floating breakwaters are usually designed with empirical relationships that don't fully
capture nonlinear wave–structure interaction or mooring effects. In this poster I use a
calibrated **Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH)** wave tank to study wave transmission
across a range of `B/L` ratios for **fixed, free-floating and moored** configurations, and
compare the results against the classical `C_t`–`B/L` design curves.

**Key finding.** Structural motion and mooring stiffness matter a lot: freely moving and
compliant systems increase transmission through radiated wave energy — something the simple
`B/L` relationships miss.
