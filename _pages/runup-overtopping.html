Wave Run-up & Overtopping

EurOtop (2018) · Iribarren number, R2% run-up and mean overtopping on sloping structures
← Tools📄 Theory (PDF)

Enter the design wave, the structure slope and the crest freeboard. The tool computes the Iribarren (surf-similarity) number and breaker type, the 2% run-up height R2%, and the mean wave overtopping discharge q, then compares q with EurOtop tolerable limits. Roughness and wave obliquity are included through the influence factors γf and γβ. See the theory note for the equations and a worked example.
