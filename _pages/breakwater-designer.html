Breakwater Designer

Conceptual design of coastal structures · hydraulic & structural response
← Tools📄 Theory (PDF)

An integrated conceptual-design tool for rubble-mound and vertical (caisson) breakwaters, built from published design formulae (Rock Manual, EurOtop, Goda, Van der Meer, d'Angremond). Set the design conditions and geometry once; the dashboard summarises the key responses and each tab gives the detail. For conceptual design only — see the theory note for formulae, validity limits and references.
