Wave Forces on Structures

Goda (vertical wall / caisson) & Morison (slender pile)
← Tools📄 Theory (PDF)

Two standard wave-force methods in one tool. Goda gives the wave-pressure distribution and horizontal force / uplift on a vertical wall or caisson breakwater; Morison gives the drag + inertia force on a slender vertical pile. See the theory note for equations and worked examples.

Inputs

Design wave Hmax = min(1.8·Hs, 0.78·h). d = h means no rubble berm.
Slender pile: D/L < ~0.2 (Morison valid). Typical Cd≈0.7–1.2, Cm≈1.5–2.0.
