Wind-Wave Prediction

SPM (1984) JONSWAP method · fetch- and duration-limited deep-water wave growth
← Tools📄 Theory (PDF)

Estimate the deep-water significant wave height Hm0 and peak period Tp generated by a wind of speed U blowing over a fetch F for a duration t. The tool applies the SPM (1984) JONSWAP growth relations, decides whether the sea is fetch-limited, duration-limited or fully developed, and reports the governing wave. See the theory note for the equations and a worked example.

Inputs

Deep-water, open-coast estimate. Wind is adjusted to the wind-stress factor UA = 0.71·U1.23 (SPM 1984).
