Partial Standing Waves & Reflection

Dean & Dalrymple §4.6 · incident + reflected waves, envelope and reflection coefficient
← Tools📄 Theory (PDF)

A wave partly reflected by a structure combines with the incident wave to form a partial standing wave (partial clapotis). Enter the incident wave and a reflection coefficient Kr (or pick a structure) to get the antinode and node heights, the node/antinode spacing, and the reflected-energy fraction. You can also recover Kr from a measured envelope. See the theory note for the derivation.

Wave-height distribution H(x)/Hi

Height oscillates between (1−Kr) at nodes and (1+Kr) at antinodes, spaced L/4 apart.

Energy split

Fraction of incident wave energy reflected (Kr²) versus transmitted or dissipated (1−Kr²).
η(x,t) = (Hi/2)cos(kx+ωt) + (KrHi/2)cos(kx−ωt);   H(x) = Hi√(1 + Kr² + 2Krcos 2kx);   Hmax=(1+Kr)Hi, Hmin=(1−Kr)Hi;   Kr = (Hmax−Hmin)/(Hmax+Hmin).
