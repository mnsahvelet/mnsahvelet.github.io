Armour Stone Sizing

Hudson (SPM 1984) & Van der Meer (1988) · rubble-mound armour unit sizing
← Tools📄 Theory (PDF)

Size the primary armour of a rubble-mound breakwater or revetment. The tool applies the Hudson stability formula (with a stability coefficient KD for the armour type) and, for rock armour, the Van der Meer (1988) formulae with notional permeability, damage level and storm duration. It returns the median mass and nominal diameter, and reports the governing (larger) design. See the theory note for equations and a worked example.

Median mass: Hudson vs Van der Meer

The larger of the two governs the rock-armour design.
Hudson:  M50 = ρrH³ / (KD·Δ³·cotα),  Δ = ρr/ρw − 1.   Van der Meer (plunging):  Hs/(ΔDn50) = 6.2·P0.18(S/√N)0.2·ξm−0.5.
